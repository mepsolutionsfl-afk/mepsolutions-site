MEP Engineering for Boca Raton's Luxury Market

Boca Raton is home to some of Palm Beach County's most exclusive gated communities — from Broken Sound and Woodfield Country Club to The Oaks and St. Andrews. These communities have strict architectural and engineering standards. Our MEP plans are designed to satisfy both the City of Boca Raton's building department and the HOA review boards that oversee these communities.

City of Boca Raton Building Dept.

Boca Raton has its own permitting authority with specific documentation requirements. We prepare packages formatted for the city's plan review process.

Gated Community Standards

HOA architectural review boards in Boca often require engineered drawings before approving renovations. Our stamped plans satisfy both HOA and city requirements.

Luxury Custom Homes

Large custom estates in Boca require advanced HVAC zoning, whole-house electrical design, and complex plumbing systems. We're comfortable with the scope.

Energy Compliance

Florida Energy Code compliance is required on all new construction and major renovations. We include energy calculations and compliance documentation with every permit set.
