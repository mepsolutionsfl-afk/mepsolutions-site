What Is Included in an MEP Plan?

An MEP plan combines the mechanical, electrical, and plumbing systems that allow a home to function safely, efficiently, and in compliance with building codes. These plans serve as the roadmap for contractors during construction and provide local building departments with the information needed to review and approve permit applications.

A complete residential MEP package typically includes HVAC layouts and equipment schedules, electrical power and lighting plans, panel schedules, load calculations, plumbing supply and drainage layouts, gas piping (when applicable), and detailed notes that ensure every system works together. Proper coordination between these disciplines helps prevent costly conflicts during construction and streamlines the overall building process.
