MEP Engineering Built for Broward County

Fort Lauderdale's "Venice of America" landscape means a large share of residential projects involve waterfront properties, seawalls, and marine environments. Salt air, tidal flooding considerations, and Broward County's permitting process all shape how MEP systems must be designed and documented here.

Broward County Permitting

We prepare drawings to Broward County Building Division standards, including all required forms, energy compliance documents, and product cut sheets.

Waterfront & Marine Environments

Canal-front and ocean-adjacent homes require corrosion-resistant equipment specifications and careful system placement. We design with Fort Lauderdale's environment in mind.

Luxury Neighborhoods

From Las Olas Isles and Harbor Beach to Rio Vista and Lauderdale-by-the-Sea, we have experience with the high-specification systems these communities require.

Renovations & Additions

Many Fort Lauderdale homes are mid-century originals being updated to luxury standards. We specialize in MEP upgrades that integrate with existing structures and pass current code.
